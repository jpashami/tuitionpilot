// How it works

The family sets the rules once. The agent does the rest.

Parent

Funds the family wallet, sets rules

Approved school, cap per term, payment window, autopay.

Student

Uploads the fee invoice

PDF, photo or text.

TuitionPilot agent

Reads, checks, signs

Claude reads the invoice. 8 rules run in code. It verifies the payment request, then signs its half.

GoBTC Pay

Co-signs in seconds

2-of-3 wallet: funds are committed the moment GoBTC co-signs. Settles on Bitcoin later.

University

Receives CAD

Converts at a rate locked at request and shown to everyone. Never has to hold crypto.

Proof The family and the school get the same receipt: payment ID, locked rate, network fee, committed and settled times.

// Why it’s safe

The AI decides what the invoice says. Code decides what gets paid.

Mandate

Rules in code, checked twice

Payee pinned by the parent, student, currency, term cap, payment window, autopay. Re-checked right before signing; the model can’t override it.

Idempotency

One payment per invoice

Order key hashed from the invoice, database claim before paying, GoBTC’s externalId. A network error means reconcile, never re-submit.

Paid vs settled

Committed in seconds, final in ~12 h

“Paid” = GoBTC co-signed, so no double spend: enough to meet the deadline. The final receipt waits for on-chain settlement.

Exchange rate

Locked, verified, shown

CAD amount priced in sats at request, locked 30 min. The agent checks the transaction amount matches before signing.
