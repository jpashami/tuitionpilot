The problem

Paying tuition from abroad breaks in three places.

Access

Money can’t always leave

Some banks ration or refuse foreign exchange for study abroad. Where wires work, they take 1–5 days and hide a 2–4% margin.

Deadlines

Late means holds

A missed due date brings late fees and registration holds — and study-permit stress for international students.

CAD 125K+

Fraud targets families

Lost by 23 students in one Canadian tuition scam. Parents far away can’t easily tell a real invoice from a fake.

University of Regina, 2025
Stablecoins

Crypto is held, not usable

India and Nigeria lead crypto adoption, mostly stablecoins used for payments. Almost no university accepts crypto directly.

The family sets the rules once. The agent does the rest.

Why it’s safe

The AI reads the invoice. Code decides what gets paid.

Mandate

Rules in code, checked twice

Pinned payee, student, currency, not already paid, term cap, payment window, autopay — re-checked right before signing. The model can’t override them, and an invoice can’t change them.

Pay once

One payment per invoice

An order key derived from the invoice, a database claim before paying, and GoBTC’s idempotent requests. A network error means reconcile, never re-submit.

Paid vs settled

Committed in seconds, final in ~12 h

“Paid” means GoBTC co-signed, so the coins can’t be spent twice — enough to meet the deadline. The final receipt waits for settlement on Bitcoin.

Exchange rate

Locked, verified, shown

The CAD amount is priced in sats at request and locked for 30 minutes. The agent checks the transaction amount before signing.

Real-world fit

Not a cheaper wire. Access, guardrails and proof.

We don’t claim to beat specialist FX providers, and we don’t promise the best exchange rate. We win where the wire fails, and we show a locked rate everyone can see.
